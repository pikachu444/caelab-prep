# CAD meshing architecture and acceptance criteria

This design covers backend candidates M1, M2 and M4-H0 and the independent solver evidence gate M3-F0. A face-conforming hybrid is useful as a neutral mesh even when a particular solver cannot consume its pyramid. Actual release scope is recorded in [CURRENT_STATE](../CURRENT_STATE.md). The [feasibility roadmap](mesher-feasibility-roadmap.md) and [Round 5 results](mesher-evidence/2026-11-04/round5/README.md) distinguish SDK experiments, reproducible geometry families and application delivery.

## Hybrid construction and its rejection cases

1. Read closed, consistently oriented trimmed solids with length units. Record each solid's identity, inner shells, oriented CAD faces and OCC mass (volume in length³). Work per owning solid. In conformal multipart mode a shared CAD face has one triangulation and identical node IDs. In independent mode maintain distinct topological namespaces for touching parts. Reciprocal contact is a solver relation and needs a separate declaration.
2. Use a uniform Cartesian grid of pitch *s* for H0. For a candidate centre classify inside the owning solid and bound the **distance to the entire trimmed boundary**, including all holes and pocket walls. A sufficient test is exact boundary distance greater than √3·s/2 + margin + kernel tolerance. The ball containing the voxel then lies in the material by the 1-Lipschitz distance property. Vertex-only and centre-only tests miss a cavity entirely inside a voxel. OCC distance to a trimmed face includes its boundary. An underlying untrimmed surface projection alone is insufficient. Alternatively certify the complete solid intersection and clearance geometrically. Keep the face-connected admissible cells, checking every exterior edge has two incident boundary quads and every boundary vertex has a single circular link. Resolve pinches deterministically by trimming and recomputing incidence. Record candidates discarded, component policy and why. An empty core yields an explicit whole-solid Tet fallback when permitted or a diagnostic. Bound the voxel budget before allocation.
3. For each exposed face use its *outward* cyclic hex-node order as the geometric pyramid base and put one new apex along the outward normal at height *h*. Choose 0 < *h* < *s*/2 and certify the whole pyramid against CAD. For example h ≤ s/4 places the apex inside the centre's circumscribed voxel ball and convexity then includes the entire pyramid. Each pyramid fits in the empty face-neighbour voxel. Within one such voxel, opposing pyramids have disjoint interiors. For orthogonal source faces x=0, y=0 the respective interiors require y > sx/(2h) and x > sy/(2h); since s/(2h)>1 these are incompatible. Rotation/reflection covers the other pairs. This handles the re-entrant valley as well as convex edges. Boundary contact must be limited to existing shared subentities. Verify these assumptions on the constructed grid and the configured height; arbitrary displaced apices require explicit intersection tests. Height lower bounds follow the chosen quality threshold. Remove offending hex cells and rebuild *all* newly exposed faces, or reject. Splitting just one quad creates a hanging interface. A nonuniform octree would need a separate balanced, conforming transition construction.
4. Replace each exposed quad in the tetrahedral cavity boundary by the four oriented triangles of its single Pyramid5. Use a watertight CAD surface triangulation, including inner CAD shells. Reverse the closed hex-plus-pyramid surface relative to material so it bounds a cavity. Build a piecewise linear complex with **every** outer triangle, cavity facet and original corner ID fixed across volume filling. One implementation route is a discrete boundary plus Gmsh built-in volume with one oriented surface loop per geometrical shell. An OCC volume and unrelated discrete entities do not define a Boolean difference. Validate closed, orientable two-manifold edge and vertex links, positive signed volumes, nesting and pairwise disjoint shells up front. A shell entirely inside another empty cavity cannot be treated as an ordinary exterior region. Verify final skin and interface triangle sets and manifold degrees after constrained tetrahedralization. A changed diagonal or inserted vertex on any fixed facet invalidates the hybrid. Preserve correspondence to CAD face/part when moving to a discrete representation. [Gmsh SDK, discrete entities and tutorials t13/x2](https://gmsh.info/doc/texinfo/gmsh.html)
5. Accept a candidate only after counting every cell, checking ordered face cycles, per-family quality and volume. Each interior quad belongs to two Hex8 or one Hex8 + one Pyramid5. Every pyramid side triangle belongs to exactly one Tet4 and one Pyramid5. Every remaining interior triangle has two owners and every exterior triangle equals one prescribed skin facet. Interior owners traverse a face in opposite order up to cyclic rotation. No repeated local IDs, duplicate cells, coincident distinct IDs within an owning solid or unmatched internal faces. Detecting face count ≤2 alone misses hanging nodes. Split-face overlaps or cracks survive that test. Compare against the entire prescribed skin, then certify nonoverlap by construction and verify all region volumes. Report failures with entity IDs. Do not publish a partly filled hybrid.

## CAD tetrahedra, layers and sweep blocks

M1 accepts explicit length limits and curvature sampling in elements per complete circle. Preserve geometric units, feature IDs and assembly policy. Size functions on a shared face must agree. If a growth limit is promised, specify the actual distance field or propagation rule and report observed neighbouring-size ratios. Minimum and maximum clamping alone impose no gradation bound. For a Wedge6 boundary layer, extrude a triangular source with common node IDs through the requested thickness progression, then recover exactly its interface in the tet interior. Limit extrusion by opposite-wall clearance, normals, side closure and Jacobian along the layer. Reject self-crossings, caps colliding with pockets and ambiguous CAD normals with affected face IDs. An explicit prepared prismatic volume is a reproducible restricted first layer case. Gmsh's 2D boundary-layer size field alone supplies no 3D prism contract. [Gmsh boundary layers and sizing](https://gmsh.info/doc/texinfo/gmsh.html)

M2 preserves existing linear planar recipe schemas. New swept blocks require matched source and target topology, compatible oriented seeds around every shared rim and a single geometric owner of each shared block face. Use oriented curve parameters for progression, symmetric spacing for bump and reproducible section frames on arcs/helices. Taper or rotate through intermediate sections, with fold and self-contact checks at tight curvature. Interface equality includes every face node and face cycle. A collapsed cross-section, incompatible intervals, ambiguous guide or changing topology requires partition or explicit fallback. For second order, define one midside ID per global edge, project CAD-boundary nodes on their intended curves/faces, and check the Jacobian across the curved element after projection. Hex20 is the incomplete quadratic hex, distinct from Hex27. Straight midside elevation must be identified as such.

## Solver boundary

Map ordered neutral types only after the specific executable, keyword, property, material, integration and patch response are established. Unverified Pyramid5 denies the **entire** structural deck. Probe CalculiX C3D5 and Radioss /PYRA5 directly. A repeated-node C3D8 or /BRICK changes interpolation or mass distribution. /PENTA6 is a thick-shell formulation requiring its own admissibility decision. Keep a parser rejection as evidence. A supported-family companion case cannot replace the requested native mixed patch. For small-strain rigid rotation prescribe the infinitesimal skew displacement or enable consistent finite kinematics for a finite rigid rotation. Measure interior equilibrium and stress, support reactions, and elastic/kinetic/contact/hourglass work with consistent units and loading rate. Native diagnostics and the local matrix govern export.

## Pre-implementation decisions from geometric review

The H0 worker can start with one closed OCC solid containing one or more void shells. For that restricted route retain a connected manifold core with one boundary component, or return its diagnosed fallback. Distinguish each CAD shell by face connectivity and check orientation/nesting. A conformal multipart hybrid needs a shared skin before independent per-solid cavity fills. Running H0 separately on touching STEP parts with independently generated triangles cannot establish conformity. The ordered Pyramid5 connectivity starts with the hex face's outward cycle. The pyramid's own outward **base face** walks this cycle in reverse.

**Faceted skin clearance is a separate condition.** A planar chord can intrude into material past the exact CAD margin. Before cavity filling test the actual closed core-plus-pyramid shell against every component of the faceted skin for intersection, including touch within geometric tolerance, and classify nesting in that same faceted solid. An equivalent certified surface displacement bound smaller than the whole-core clearance is sufficient. A global signed volume comparison alone can hide opposing errors. Validate cell quality against the configured per-family floor and report distance from that floor. Merely positive Jacobians accept arbitrarily bad slivers.

The M1 scoped layer is a planar triangular wall in a certified straight swept solid. A distance/size field growing away from curved faces and clamped to the requested bounds expresses a target size gradient. Adjacent-cell growth requires a separate measured ratio. For M2, opposite edges on each six-faced block impose equivalences on division counts. Propagate these across every shared face, map geometric direction to signed OCC curve parameter, then check the ordered coordinates of the generated seed nodes against requested progression or bump. Curvature, twist and taper require prepared six-faced block topology. General automatic partition and nonplanar boundary inflation require separate demonstrations. The gate owner observed CalculiX 2.21 reject C3D5 before the solve, so keep every C3D5 mixed-deck solve blocked on that executable.

### Automatic partition for a stepped annulus

With `structured_blocks=True` and `block_partition="revolved"`, the backend accepts one unpartitioned solid with a through bore on an axis parallel to Z. It recovers the axis and axial stations from horizontal full-circle trims. Each interval must have one inner and one outer cylindrical or conical wall. Radial shoulders induce cuts across the intervals containing them. A tapered cap changes the wall radius without introducing a cut through every lower interval. A shoulder that crosses a sloped wall strictly inside one block requires a different partition and the worker reports it.

Each meridional quadrilateral revolves through four sectors. A single OCC Boolean fragment includes the original solid, the sectors and the original trimmed boundary faces. Acceptance requires that the original's volume descendants equal the complete set of sector descendants, with one unique region per sector. The worker checks six faces per block and four edges per face, total exact CAD volume and one-to-many mapping of each source face to exterior block faces by Boolean history and summed trimmed area. The report preserves source face names, source and block tags, axial stations and axis coordinates. Input curve seeds on this generated topology require an explicit correspondence. The fresh-CAD route rejects those seeds, named XAO inputs, and independent-volume mode.

The unpartitioned housing STEP yielded seven meridional bands and 28 blocks at axial stations 0, 5, 7, 14 and 15 mm, with OCC volume 4336.44505950525 mm³ before partition and 4336.44505950528 mm³ after. The independent check counted 1,408 Hex8 and 2,112 nodes. An offset-axis flange with different radii and stations yielded 12 blocks. An eccentric bore was rejected on the coaxial-circle check. The prepared twisted/tapered block and the curved-edge seed tests use a separate input path. ## Face ordering, quality and CAD error

Local indices below are zero-based, in Gmsh order. For a positive reference tet 0123 with vertex 3 above oriented 012, outward triangles are 021, 013, 123, 203. For bottom 0123 and matching top 4567 of a hex, outward quads are 0321, 4567, 0154, 1265, 2376, 3047. For pyramid base 0123 and apex 4, outward faces are 0321, 014, 124, 234, 304. For bottom triangle 012 and matching top 345 of a wedge, outward faces are 021, 345, 0143, 1254, 2035. The layer's triangular cap mates with a single Tet4 face and each internal side quad mates with a complete neighbouring Wedge6 quad. Validate topological dimension, family, polynomial order and cyclic rotations at import. Sorting is sufficient for a face hash, after which reverse winding still has to be checked. Higher-order faces also require the same midside IDs on the same edges. Corners alone cannot detect incompatible quadratic interfaces.

For each solid and family, record population, failed count, worst value, controlling element ID and quality definition. The advanced CAD worker's `min_scaled_jacobian` is the minimum signed determinant of incident unit-length corner edges, evaluated at the base corners for pyramids. On a surface this uses a signed area-normal component. The separate `min_sdk_scaled_jacobian` is Gmsh's sampled `minSJ`. The worker also reports Gmsh's sampled signed inverse condition number in `min_sicn` and the adaptive minimum determinant in `min_det_jacobian`. A collapsed-coordinate pyramid has a singular reference mapping at the apex. A generic Cartesian determinant there is undefined. The affine H0 cube and planar square pyramid of strictly positive height have constructive volume-positivity checks independent of sampling. [Gmsh API, getElementQualities](https://gmsh.info/doc/texinfo/gmsh.html) · [Johnen, Remacle and Geuzaine on geometrical validity](https://gmsh.info/doc/preprints/gmsh_curved_preprint.pdf)

| Family | Dimension and shape measurements | Additional validity evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Tri3, Quad4 | Area and ordered corner angles. Use minimum/maximum included face angle in degrees and dimensionless equiangular skew. A dihedral angle is undefined for one surface element. | Positive oriented area against a known surface normal. Check the bilinear quad's signed surface mapping and warpage. Neighbouring-facet dihedral describes mesh curvature and is a different measurement. |
| Tet4 | Six edges and six interior dihedrals. Positive signed volume equals the oriented corner determinant divided by 6. A sliver can have nearly equal edges. | Report minimum dihedral together with scaled Jacobian and edge aspect ratio. |
| Hex8, Wedge6 | Twelve and nine edges respectively. Evaluate the appropriate trilinear hex or triangular-prism mapping. Use actual triangular and quadrilateral faces for angles and identify how normals are evaluated on warped quads. | Sign changes within a warped element can escape corner tests. Check minimum determinant and integrated signed volume. Thin high-aspect wedges may be desired for a layer; record aspect and application threshold by family. |
| Pyramid5 | Eight edges, four triangular sides and one quadrilateral base. In H0 volume equals square-base area × perpendicular height/3. | Correct base winding plus apex height and a normalized Jacobian floor protect the hex/tet transition. Evaluate side-to-side and base-to-side dihedrals. |
| Tet10, Hex20 | Corner edge lengths, face angles and corner dihedrals describe the linear hull. The actual geometry uses all 10 or 20 nodes. | Integrate the actual isoparametric determinant, examine interior and boundary mapping including curved edges and require a reliable minimum-determinant check. Report unsupported curvature checks as unresolved. |

For reproducible cross-family reporting an **edge aspect ratio** can be defined as longest/shortest physical corner edge. Identify it as an edge ratio when a solver uses a different aspect metric. One possible **face-angle skewness** is the worst of (θ−θ₀)/(180°−θ₀) and (θ₀−θ)/θ₀ over face corners, with θ₀ = 60° for a triangle and 90° for a quad. The **interior dihedral** at a convex straight edge shared by outward unit face normals n₁,n₂ is 180°−acos(clamp(n₁·n₂,−1,1)) in degrees. Label any shell face-angle substitute explicitly. Per-family floors, angle/ratio limits and volume tolerances are test or caller inputs with defined units. Positivity is a hard validity gate. Product-specific acceptability still depends on formulation and loading. [Cubit tetrahedron metrics](https://cubit.sandia.gov/files/cubit/16.12/help_manual/WebHelp/mesh_generation/mesh_quality_assessment/tetrahedral_metrics.htm) · [Cubit wedge metrics](https://cubit.sandia.gov/files/cubit/16.04/help_manual/WebHelp/mesh_generation/mesh_quality_assessment/wedge_metrics.htm)

For H0 report two different residuals, with a fixed coordinate origin near the solid to reduce cancellation in moment sums.

1. **Partition closure:** compare the sum of positive signed cell volumes with the signed volume of the oriented faceted material boundary, including voids, from Σ a·(b×c)/6 over skin triangles. Report both absolute and relative residuals. Choose floating-point tolerance from coordinate scale and accumulation precision; inspect each solid independently.
2. **CAD approximation:** compare the same cell sum with the OCC mass of the corresponding solid. Divide by positive CAD volume for a relative error and also report absolute error in model length³. Approximating fillets, holes and curved skin with linear facets produces a genuine chord error. Set the CAD tolerance before the run and report mesh-size/chord controls. Exact cancellation of the global residual cannot establish that a bore, a shared face or a very thin wall survived.

When a gate fails, retain the selected method, owner IDs, native or mesher diagnostics and observed counts for the caller. Whole-solid Tet fallback reports its actual families, reason and the user's allowance. For patch A, use consistent uniaxial stress boundary conditions (free lateral contraction, or its equivalent affine Poisson contraction), check εzz and σzz on actual integration points and compare separate support/load resultants. If transverse strain is prescribed zero, the constrained modulus differs from Young's modulus. The solver owner's version-specific evidence controls family admission.
